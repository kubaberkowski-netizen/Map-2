# US 2,000-place draft

Generated by `node tools/harvest-us-walk-spots.js --all --target 40 --resume` and applied with `node tools/harvest-us-walk-spots.js --apply`.

The set contains exactly 40 additions for every US state. Every row has an English Wikipedia article and Wikidata entity, coordinates inside the state polygon, and a matching Wikidata administrative-state path. Candidates are screened for visitable place types, globally deduplicated against the existing catalogue and one another at 80 metres, balanced by category and locality, and backed by retained source metadata.

Discovery and structured facts use the QLever Wikidata mirror; state geometry comes from PublicaMundi; research excerpts come from English Wikipedia. App prose is a machine draft (`d`) and remains pending editorial review. `.raw/` is an untracked resumable request cache.
