🍽️ Institutions · 7

  • Café ImperialNa Poříčí, Nové Město
    A working Art Nouveau/Art Deco dining room from 1914, its walls and columns entirely tiled in ceramic mosaic, inside the Hotel Imperial.
  • FieldU Milosrdných, Staré Město
    A minimalist, glass-fronted room on a quiet Old Town street where a single seasonal tasting menu does the talking.
  • La Degustation Bohême BourgeoiseHaštalská, Staré Město
    One Michelin star for cooking that treats old Bohemian recipes -- forcemeats, offal, wild game -- as a discipline to be recovered rather than a style to be referenced.
  • U GlaubicůMalostranské náměstí, Malá Strana
    A beer cellar reportedly over 700 years old, under a townhouse on Malostranské náměstí, a few steps from St Nicholas Church.
  • U TellerůVinohradská, Vinohrady
    A Vinohrady steakhouse that ages and butchers its own meat on site, serving reworked Czech classics alongside charcoal-grilled cuts.
  • Výtopna Railway RestaurantVáclavské náměstí, Wenceslas Square
    A miniature freight train shuttles beer, wine and food along nearly a kilometre of track laid through the dining room, crossing five working drawbridges to reach every table.
  • XL BistroZlatnická, Nové Město
    A small bistro on Zlatnická, across from the Cosmopolitan Hotel, serving Czech dishes alongside a short flight of Czech fruit brandies.
